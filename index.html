See every grant at a glance: where it stands, when it's due, and what's missing before your next meeting. Then add the deadlines to your calendar.

Use your own data

Your file stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

What the app can read:

  • .xlsx (Excel). It reads the tab named Grants, or the first tab if there isn't one.
  • .csv (comma-separated values).
  • Columns needed in either file, in any order: grant_id, funder, program, deadline, status. The app skips any other columns, so keep yours wherever you like.
